Restore the full game

If you bought the full game and it is locked on a new device or after reinstalling, open Settings › Restore purchases in the game (or "Restore purchases" on the unlock card). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. Family Sharing members can restore it too.

Refunds

Purchases are sold by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Saves

Your progress is saved on your device only, at every dawn and dusk. The game has no account and no cloud save. Deleting the app deletes your progress; your purchase stays with your Apple ID.

What is free

The prologue, the map Highlands with its boss, and the Endless Vigil up to night 10 are free. One purchase unlocks three more maps, the Cinder Tiers and the Endless Vigil without a night limit. There are no ads, no timers and nothing for sale that makes you stronger. The game plays offline.

Accessibility

Settings has a Simple layout (Bram's skills cast on their own), left-handed layouts, game speed (50%, 75% or 100%), Reduce motion, three text sizes, UI scale, screen shake, haptics and separate music and sound volumes.

Privacy

The game collects no data. Read the privacy policy.

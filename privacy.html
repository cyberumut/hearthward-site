Hearthward: Night Defense · Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how Hearthward: Night Defense (the "game", for iPhone and iPad) handles personal data. A shorter version of this notice is in the game under Settings › Privacy.

Who is responsible

The game is made by Hearthward Dev, a one-person studio. The data controller is the developer named as the seller on the game's App Store page. Contact: ubacnet@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game needs no account and asks for no personal details.
  • It shows ads from Google AdMob: at most one full-screen ad every four minutes, only when you leave the result screen after a run and never during play, and an optional ad you can choose to watch for Ember ×2. The one-time "Full game" purchase removes the full-screen ads.
  • The ads are non-personalized and the game does not track you across apps or websites. It never shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency ("Allow tracking") prompt. Google's ad software in the game collects the device and usage data it needs to show and measure ads (see Advertising).
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks you first. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. The game has no server and no cloud save.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID.
  • The game has no analytics of its own and sends no gameplay data to us.

What data the game handles

Saves and settings (on your device only). Your campaign progress, unlocked items, the run in progress and your settings are stored in the game's own folder on your device. They can be included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), which Apple handles under its own terms. We cannot access them.

Purchases (handled by Apple). The "Full game" unlock is a one-time in-app purchase sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple tells the game on your device whether the unlock is owned (including through Family Sharing and after a refund); the game stores that answer in its save so it also works offline. We receive no payment details, no Apple ID and no purchase history, and the game does not pass your purchase to Google. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple's processing is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising data (collected by Google). The ads in the game come from Google AdMob. Google's ad software, built into the game, sends Google the data described under Advertising below. We do not receive that data.

Gameplay data. The game's own code does not send any gameplay statistics, crash reports or diagnostics to us or to anyone else. The game's engine (Unity) is configured so that none of its optional services (analytics, crash reporting, performance reporting, hardware statistics, engine diagnostics) are active.

If a future update adds anonymous gameplay statistics (for example which maps are played, how runs end and how long they last, to balance the game), they will only be sent if you allow it on a consent screen. They will not include your name, contact details, location or an advertising identifier, they will not be used for advertising or tracking, and you will be able to turn them off at any time under Settings › Privacy. This policy will be updated before such a change.

Support e-mails. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and whatever your message contains (for example your device model, its iOS version or a screenshot). We use it only to answer you, keep it no longer than needed for that, and never share it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Crash reports shared by Apple. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymized crash reports with us through App Store Connect. This is controlled by you and Apple.

Advertising

Who shows the ads. The ads come from Google AdMob, Google's ad service. The game contains Google's Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (for the consent form). They send data from your device directly to Google, which uses it as a separate controller under its own privacy policy. We only see aggregated reports in AdMob (for example how many ads were shown in each country), never data about you. Google is the only company the game shares data with. We confirm that Google provides the same or equal protection of this data as this policy and Apple's App Store Review Guidelines require, under Google's privacy policy and the data protection terms Google accepts for AdMob.

When ads appear.

  • A full-screen ad can appear only when you leave the result screen after a run, at most once every four minutes. Never during play, and never after the prologue or your first run.
  • On the result screen you can choose to watch a short ad for Ember ×2, which doubles that run's Ember. It is always optional, in the free and the full game alike.
  • The one-time "Full game" purchase removes the full-screen ads. The optional Ember ×2 ad stays available, and buying never doubles Ember by itself: nothing for sale makes you stronger.

What Google's ad software collects (from Google's own disclosure for its Mobile Ads SDK):

  • Device identifiers that are specific to this app or its developer (for example the identifier for vendor). Not Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA): the game never asks for tracking permission, so the IDFA is not available.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were requested, shown, tapped, watched or closed, and app launches.
  • Approximate location: your country, region or city, estimated from your IP address. Never your precise location: the game does not ask for location permission.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, performance data and other technical data about the ad software and your device (for example the device model, iOS version, app version and network type).

What it is used for. Showing ads and measuring them, limiting how often you see the same ad, preventing fraud and abuse, and diagnosing and improving the ad service.

Non-personalized, no tracking. The game asks Google for non-personalized ads only: ads chosen from the game and general context such as your approximate location, not from a profile of you. It also turns off Google's publisher first-party identifier and limits ads to content suitable for ages 9 and up. The game has no account and gives Google no name or contact details, and it does not combine this data with data from other companies' apps or websites to track you. It never shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Before any ad is requested, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to the storage of and access to information on your device and to the ad processing it describes. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent, Google may still show limited ads, a more restricted kind of non-personalized ad.

Report an ad. If you see an ad that seems inappropriate, write to ubacnet@gmail.com, ideally with a screenshot and the time you saw it. We will review it and block it in AdMob where we can.

Google's policies. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google keeps and deletes the data it receives under its own policy and may process it outside your country, including in the United States.

Legal basis

  • The local save is necessary to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract / legitimate interest).
  • Answering a support e-mail you send us rests on our legitimate interest in helping you.
  • Ads in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: your consent, given in Google's consent form, for the storage of and access to information on your device and for the ad processing you allow. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices.
  • Ads elsewhere: our legitimate interest in funding the free part of the game with non-personalized ads. The full-screen ads end with the one-time purchase, and the Ember ×2 ad is shown only when you choose it.
  • Any future gameplay statistics would rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Children

The game is not directed to children. Its App Store age rating is 9+, and its ads are limited to content suitable for ages 9 and up (parental guidance). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The game asks for no personal details, and its ads are not personalized.

Keeping and deleting your data

All game data lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes your saves, your settings and your ad consent choice. A purchase stays with your Apple ID and can be restored with "Restore purchases" in the game. Data already sent to Google for ads is kept and deleted by Google under its privacy policy.

Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the UK GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) you have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Board, Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu). Because the game keeps your data on your device only, we normally hold nothing about you (apart from any e-mail you sent us); contact us and we will answer any request. The advertising data is held by Google under device identifiers, not under your name (the game has none): you can withdraw your ad consent in the game, and Google answers requests about its own processing (policies.google.com/privacy).

Material changes will also be shown in the game before they take effect.

Hearthward: Night Defense · Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Hearthward: Night Defense (the "game", for iPhone and iPad) handles personal data. A shorter version of this notice is in the game under Settings › Privacy.

Who is responsible

The game is made by Hearthward Dev, a one-person studio. The data controller is the developer named as the seller on the game's App Store page. Contact: ubacnet@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game needs no account and asks for no personal details.
  • It shows no ads, contains no advertising or tracking software and does not track you across apps or websites. It never shows Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt because it does not track.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. The game has no server and no cloud save.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID.
  • The game sends no gameplay data anywhere.

What data the game handles

Saves and settings (on your device only). Your campaign progress, unlocked items, the run in progress and your settings are stored in the game's own folder on your device. They can be included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), which Apple handles under its own terms. We cannot access them.

Purchases (handled by Apple). The "Full game" unlock is a one-time in-app purchase sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple tells the game on your device whether the unlock is owned (including through Family Sharing and after a refund); the game stores that answer in its save so it also works offline. We receive no payment details, no Apple ID and no purchase history. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple's processing is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Gameplay data. The game does not send any gameplay statistics, crash reports or diagnostics to us or to anyone else. The game's engine (Unity) is configured so that none of its optional services (analytics, crash reporting, performance reporting, hardware statistics, engine diagnostics) are active.

If a future update adds anonymous gameplay statistics (for example which maps are played, how runs end and how long they last, to balance the game), they will only be sent if you allow it on a consent screen. They will not include your name, contact details, location or an advertising identifier, they will not be used for advertising or tracking, and you will be able to turn them off at any time under Settings › Privacy. This policy will be updated before such a change.

Support e-mails. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and whatever your message contains (for example your device model, its iOS version or a screenshot). We use it only to answer you, keep it no longer than needed for that, and never share it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Crash reports shared by Apple. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymized crash reports with us through App Store Connect. This is controlled by you and Apple.

Legal basis

The local save is necessary to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract / legitimate interest). Answering a support e-mail you send us rests on our legitimate interest in helping you. We process no other personal data. Any future gameplay statistics would rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Children

The game is for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Because the game collects no personal data, no parental consent is needed to play.

Keeping and deleting your data

All game data lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes your saves and settings. A purchase stays with your Apple ID and can be restored with "Restore purchases" in the game.

Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the UK GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) you have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Board, Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu). Because the game keeps your data on your device only, we normally hold nothing about you (apart from any e-mail you sent us); contact us and we will answer any request.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game's data handling changes (for example before any analytics are added) and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be shown in the game before they take effect.
